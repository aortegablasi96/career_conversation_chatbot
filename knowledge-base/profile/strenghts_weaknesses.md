### Summary of Weaknesses:

1. Being too direct in communication

- Weakness: My communication style is naturally very direct and transparent.
- Impact: While it’s efficient, it can sometimes be perceived as too strong, especially in multicultural environments or with more sensitive stakeholders.
- What I do to improve: I adapt my tone depending on the audience, focusing more on context, listening, and asking questions before pushing conclusions.
- Example: During vendor coordination at Ponera Group, I learned to structure feedback more collaboratively, which improved the relationship with external partners and made delivery smoother.

2. High standards / demanding mindset

- Weakness: I have high standards for quality and delivery, and I can be demanding when timelines are tight.
- Impact: This can create pressure on teams or lead to spending too much time optimizing instead of prioritizing the highest-impact tasks.
- What I do to improve: I apply a stronger prioritization mindset and focus on “value first,” using MVP logic and clear acceptance criteria.
- Example: While managing SaaS product development as Product Owner, I started using MoSCoW prioritization and release planning to avoid over-engineering and keep delivery efficient.

3. Over-focusing on outcomes over team pace

- Weakness: I’m strongly results-oriented, and I sometimes underestimate change fatigue or workload when pushing ambitious delivery goals.
- Impact: This can create stress in teams or reduce sustainability if not managed properly.
- What I do to improve: I actively monitor capacity, create realistic milestones, and encourage open communication around workload constraints.
- Example: In ABB’s global setup, I improved execution by adjusting planning cycles to match regional capacity, which improved collaboration and reduced bottlenecks.
